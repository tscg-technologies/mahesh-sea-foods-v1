B2B Wholesale Procurement

Harbor-Direct Bulk Seafood, Empowering Local Businesses

Mahesh Sea Foods is Hyderabad's dedicated B2B seafood procurement partner. We procure bulk marine fish and prawns directly from coastal landing jetties in Visakhapatnam, Chennai, and Kochi, delivering daily morning crates to local seafood stalls, retail shops, and commercial kitchens.

Our Wholesale Mission

Direct Harbor Sourcing Built for Local Seafood Retailers

For years, local fish stall owners, supermarkets, and restaurants across Hyderabad had to navigate multiple tiers of middlemen, inflated prices, uncertain morning arrival times, and water-glaze weight manipulation.

Mahesh Sea Foods operates as a direct bridge between major coastal boat landing jetties and local seafood businesses. Every consignment is packed immediately in insulated crates with clean crushed ice dockside, transported through overnight cold chain, and unpacked at our Uppal depot before dawn.

"హార్బర్ నుండి నేరుగా మీ దుకాణానికి... నికరమైన నాణ్యత, నిజమైన లాభం!"

Direct coastal harbor supply gives your local seafood business uncompromised quality, dependable supply, and healthy daily retail margins.

Wholesale Cold-Chain & Logistics

Built for Local Seafood Retailers & Kitchens

How our wholesale infrastructure guarantees higher retail margins, exact net weight, and zero-headache morning deliveries:

🚢

Multi-Port Procurement

Direct procurement contracts at primary boat landing jetties in Visakhapatnam, Chennai, and Kochi. Sourcing diversity ensures steady supply even when one coast experiences monsoon bans.

Primary Hubs: Vizag • Chennai • Kochi
⏱️

Dawn Arrival & Early Dispatch

Overnight express cold trucks reach our Uppal wholesale depot before dawn. Insulated iced crates are sorted, weighed, and ready for local retail stall collection before morning customer footfall begins.

Timings: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (Monday to Saturday)

100% Net Weight Assurance

We eliminate common industry trade tricks like water-glaze dipping and excessive ice tare weight. You pay only for genuine seafood weight with certified count grades on every prawn and fish crate.

Standard Crate Sizes: 10kg • 20kg • 30kg • 50kg
